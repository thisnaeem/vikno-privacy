Vikno — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026 · Developer: Muhammad Naeem Anjum · Contact: naeemanjum076@gmail.com

What Vikno does

Vikno is a screen recording and screenshot studio for Windows. It records your screen, a window, or a region (with microphone and system audio), takes screenshots — including scrolling captures — and lets you annotate images and edit videos in its built-in editors. Everything is processed entirely on your own PC: Vikno works fully offline and has no cloud component of any kind.

Data we collect

None. Vikno has no user accounts, we run no servers for it, and the app contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising, or crash-reporting tools. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

Screen, microphone, camera, and system audio

Vikno captures only when you start a capture, and only what you choose to capture:

Screen. Your screen, a window, or a region is captured only while a recording you started is running, or at the moment you take a screenshot. Vikno never captures in the background.

Microphone. Your microphone is used only during a recording, and only if microphone audio is enabled for that recording.

Camera. Your webcam is used only if you enable the camera overlay for a recording.

System audio. During a recording, Vikno can capture your PC's own audio output (for example, the sound of a video or a call playing on your computer) using the standard Windows loopback mechanism. This captures only your own device's output, only during a recording you started.

All captured audio and video is written only to files on your PC, in the save location you choose.

Information stored on your PC

Your recordings, screenshots, and edited media are stored in the folder you pick in Settings. Vikno's media library database, your settings, and your keyboard shortcuts are stored locally in Vikno's data folder on your PC. During a recording, Vikno also stores your cursor positions and clicks together with that recording, so the editor can offer its auto-zoom feature; this data stays on your PC and is deleted when you delete the recording. None of this information ever leaves your device. You can delete your media at any time from within the app, and uninstalling Vikno removes the app; its local data folder can be deleted like any other folder.

Text recognition (OCR)

Vikno's OCR feature, which extracts text from screenshots, runs locally on your PC using the bundled open-source Tesseract engine. Your images are never uploaded for processing.

When Vikno uses the internet

Only for app updates, and only in the standalone version:

App updates. The standalone version of Vikno checks GitHub Releases for new versions and downloads updates from GitHub when you install them. As with any download, GitHub can see your IP address and which file was requested, under its own privacy policy. If you installed Vikno from the Microsoft Store, updates are delivered by the Microsoft Store instead, and Microsoft's own policies apply to that process; the Store version contains no update checker of its own.

Vikno has no other network use: it downloads nothing else, uploads nothing, and needs no internet connection to work.

Permissions

Vikno uses microphone access (for recordings with microphone audio), camera access (for the optional webcam overlay), screen capture (its core function), and internet access (for the standalone version's update check described above). The microphone and camera capabilities are declared in its Microsoft Store package.

Children

Vikno is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13.
